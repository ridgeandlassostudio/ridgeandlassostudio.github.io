1. Who we are

Arrow Out Puzzle - Tap Escape (the “Game”, Android package com.randlstudio.arrowoutpuzzle) is made by Ridge and Lasso Studio, an independent developer based in India (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at ridgeandlassostudio@gmail.com.

This policy explains what data the Game uses, why, who else receives it, and the choices you have. It is kept short on purpose: the Game has no accounts of its own, no servers of ours and no analytics tools.

2. Data that stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, times, streaks, awards, daily challenge results), settings (sound, vibration, theme, language, reminders) and whether you bought Remove ads are stored only on your phone. We never receive this data.

You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the Game.

4. Ads and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch Google’s consent form asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Manage ad choices.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android phones). Ads will then not be personalised from your past activity.
  • US state privacy laws: where showing personalised ads counts as “selling” or “sharing” data, deleting your advertising ID, or turning on your browser or device opt-out signal, opts you out.
  • Buying Remove ads stops all interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads (for an extra heart or hint) only play when you tap them.

Google and its certified ad partners act under their own policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Notifications

Reminders (for example, that today’s daily puzzle is ready or your streak is at risk) are scheduled on your phone itself. No data is sent anywhere to deliver them. Turn them off in Settings → Notifications or in your phone’s app settings.

6. Emails you send us

If you write to ridgeandlassostudio@gmail.com (for example with Settings → Write to us), we receive your email address and what you choose to send, such as your phone model. We use it only to answer you, and delete it within 24 months unless we need it longer to resolve a dispute.

7. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Contract: to provide the Game, purchases, saving and restoring progress.
  • Consent: for personalised ads and the ad cookies or identifiers that need it. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: showing non-personalised ads to fund a free game, keeping it secure, fixing crashes and answering support emails.

8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal data for money and do not share it with anyone other than the Google services listed above. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or users’ safety.

9. Children

The Game is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us data (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

10. Retention

  • On-device data: until you reset progress, clear app data or uninstall.
  • Play Games data and saved progress: until you delete it in the Google Play Games app or your Google account.
  • Ad data: kept by Google as described in its policy.
  • Support emails: up to 24 months.

11. International transfers and security

Google may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, using safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. Data sent between the Game and Google is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the data we handle to the minimum.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, or US state laws such as California’s CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to appeal our decision or complain to your data protection authority.

Because we do not hold your game data, most requests are fastest through the controls above or through Google (myaccount.google.com). For anything else, email ridgeandlassostudio@gmail.com. We reply within one month (45 days for US requests) and will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how the Game uses data, we will update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the Game when the change is significant.
