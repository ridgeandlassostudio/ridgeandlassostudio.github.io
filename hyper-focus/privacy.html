01

Who we are

Hyper Focus: Minimalist Launcher (the “App”) is an Android minimalist launcher developed and published by Ridge & Lasso Studio, an independent app and game developer based in India (“we”, “us”, “our”).

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how the App handles information, you can reach us at ridgeandlassostudio@gmail.com.

02

100% on-device data

We do not operate user accounts, cloud sync servers, or remote analytics databases for Hyper Focus. All information processed by the App stays 100% on your device:

  • App usage stats: Screen-time totals, daily usage summaries, and app time limits are read and calculated locally on your phone.
  • Foreground app detection (Accessibility / Deep Block): Active app checks for Deep Block, mindful pauses, and focus rules happen locally in real time and are never recorded to remote servers.
  • Held notifications: Notifications buffered or batched during focus sessions or quiet schedules remain strictly inside your device’s local storage.
  • Schedules, favorites & settings: Your focus schedules, pinned favorites, hidden apps, themes, and launcher preferences are saved locally on your device.

None of this data is ever uploaded, shared with third parties, or sold.

03

Optional Android permissions

All advanced permissions in Hyper Focus are completely optional and may be granted or revoked at any time in your Android system settings. Each permission is used strictly for its corresponding on-device feature:

Permission On-device feature & strict purpose
Usage Access
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS		 Reads app usage statistics locally so you can view screen-time insights and mindful usage limits.
Display over other apps
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW		 Displays mindful pause prompts, intention checks, or focus block overlays over selected apps.
Accessibility Service
BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE		 Detects the current foreground app locally to enforce Deep Block and app-launch friction rules. It never reads screen text, passwords, keystrokes, or personal messages, and never sends data off your device.
Notification Access
BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE		 Holds and batches non-essential notifications locally during focus sessions or scheduled quiet hours so you can review them later.
Calendar
READ_CALENDAR		 Reads upcoming calendar events locally to show your schedule/agenda on the minimalist home screen.
Location
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION		 Used strictly to power the optional local weather display on your home screen when enabled, never for tracking, profiling, or advertising.
04

Payments & Google Play Billing

Paid plans, pro unlocks, or subscriptions in Hyper Focus are handled directly by Google Play Billing. Payment methods, billing addresses, taxes, and receipts are processed by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy.

We never receive, access, or store your payment card numbers, UPI details, or bank information. The App receives only purchase verification and entitlement status from Google Play.

05

No ads, trackers, or data sales

Hyper Focus: Minimalist Launcher includes no third-party advertising SDKs and no behavioral tracking SDKs. We never sell, rent, or trade your data.

06

Support emails you send us

If you email us at ridgeandlassostudio@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use this information only to reply to your inquiry or troubleshoot your issue, and we never add you to marketing lists.

07

Data retention & deletion

Because all launcher data (usage caches, held notifications, schedules, favorites, and settings) resides locally on your device, you control it directly. You can clear app data in Android Settings, revoke optional permissions, or uninstall the App to delete all local data permanently.

Support emails are kept only as long as necessary to address your request or comply with legal requirements, and you may ask us to delete your email correspondence at any time.

08

Children’s privacy

Hyper Focus: Minimalist Launcher is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal details by email, please contact us so we can delete them.

09

Your rights

Depending on your location (including India, the EEA, the UK, and other jurisdictions), you may have rights to access, correct, or erase personal data. Because Hyper Focus keeps your app data 100% on-device, you can manage or delete it directly on your phone. For any support emails we hold, contact ridgeandlassostudio@gmail.com.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new features or legal requirements. When we make updates, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
